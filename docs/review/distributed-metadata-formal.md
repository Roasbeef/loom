# Conditional metadata model review, 2026-10-04

The metadata model now separates submission, durable settlement and caller
observation. A timeout leaves the original conditional operation live and its
caller uncertain. The checked graph includes settlement before a timeout,
settlement after a minority timeout/healing, successful reply loss, late reply
delivery, missing-receipt observation before settlement, and same-ID
reconciliation. These schedules reflect the supplied three-node metadata
spike's timeout followed by a visible receipt, without adopting Khepri.

The change owns only the existing `distributed-authority` model directory and
this review record. `Ownership.tla`, the P remote-execution model and the Lean
admission proof/reducer bridge keep their semantics. The root orchestrator
owns production integration, public contracts and independent final review.

## Preserved obligations

The model uses one authority key and a complete conditional envelope:
incarnation, revision, ownership epoch and owner. A pending operation reserves
retained receipt capacity before transmission. Its exact retry preserves ID,
digest and predicate. Final conditional rejection is retained alongside
success. Conflicting digest representatives cannot overwrite the reserved ID
or its settled outcome. Administrative recreation advances incarnation and
both fences. Compaction preserves retained results and authority fencing.

Minority/no-quorum access cannot settle a new command or acknowledge it as
Applied. A delayed reply to a previously quorum-settled command can still
resolve a historical outcome after a partition. That receipt supplies no
current owner grant. Physical writer and native execution safety stay in the
separate ownership and execution models.

`MetadataSafety.cfg` and `MetadataSafetyCapacity.cfg` check TypeOK,
CapacityReserved, ReceiptRetained, StableOperationId, OneLogicalCommit,
NoFalseNoCommit, NoMinorityAck, MonotonicFence, ResultHasReceipt and
NoReusedIncarnation. The graph has two operation IDs, three envelope slots,
two digest equality classes, one recreation, two incarnations, epochs/revisions
1 through 4 and receipt capacity 1 or 2. Quorum access is an abstract mode;
replica log consensus is assumed. Neither model has fairness assumptions.
Unbounded delay, safe blocking and a permanently unknown result remain legal.

## Forbidden schedules and disposition

The timeout mutant commits ID 1, loses timely observation and then asserts
NoCommit. Its four-state counterexample rejects that classification. The
retry mutant gives the uncertain logical operation ID 2 and refreshes its
predicate. Its six-state witness commits the logical operation twice,
advancing the epoch to 3. Preserving only the operation payload would not
prevent that second transition: the identity and predicate must stay fixed.

The minority mutant fabricates an acknowledgement before any durable
settlement. The ABA mutant recreates the complete A/1 envelope after epoch 2
has committed, violating the retained fencing high-water mark. The receipt
mutant deletes the only reconciliable outcome during compaction. Each has a
four-state witness against its exact named invariant. The unmutated graph
checks those properties at both capacity bounds.

The model slice passes its requested local checks. Its production obligations
remain open: persist reservation/identity before submit, atomically settle
full-envelope comparison plus digest-bound final receipt, preserve rejected
results, supply authoritative reconciliation and capacity/retention bounds,
and persist tombstones/fences through physical compaction. The metadata spike
still has its native deadline/nested-timeout, snapshot/upgrade and compiled
Gleam transaction-bridge gaps. This model pass does not close the D3 adoption
gate.

No metadata implementation exists in this slice, so there is no
metadata-to-Gleam differential bridge or implementation refinement proof.
The adjacent admission bridge concerns a different reducer. The model also
does not prove a relational refinement from Metadata to Ownership, distributed
consensus, replica membership, actual snapshot/WAL behavior, corrupt storage,
Byzantine participants, payload byte/disk budgets or an eventual retention
horizon. It assumes correct atomic durability boundaries and treats compaction
as preservation of logical facts. No automatic failover or unconditional
handoff progress is claimed.

## Independent verification

The root reran the complete 26-case TLC suite against the same source hashes;
it exited 0. Ownership exhausted 965,376 distinct states, and Metadata exhausted
78,978 and 66,918 at the two capacity bounds. All witness and mutation controls
failed their exact expected invariants. A fresh independent adversarial review
found no actionable model, runner or claim defect. It confirmed that a delayed
quorum receipt remains valid historical evidence during a partition, while an
unsettled minority acknowledgement is forbidden. This review adds no metadata
implementation, storage or consensus proof.
